    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=89mm]{results/movement_onset_subthreshold_wheel.pdf}
    \caption{\textbf{Movement onset and sub-threshold wheel motion.}
    Analyses used 396 BWM sessions with locally available wheel data, comprising
    128 mice from 11 laboratories; all 396 sessions contributed to panel
    \textbf{b}.
    \textbf{a}, Example wheel traces aligned to the reported movement onset and
    sign-normalized to the eventual movement direction. The IBL algorithm
    detects a candidate movement when displacement exceeds eight encoder samples
    within 200~ms, refines its onset using a 1.5-sample displacement, and assigns
    \texttt{firstMovement\_times} to the first candidate with peak amplitude
    $\geq 0.1$~rad. Neither choice nor direction enters this definition. The
    shaded interval (20~ms immediately preceding onset) was excluded from
    pre-onset measurements to avoid onset-boundary contamination.
    \textbf{b}, Session-wise percentages of trials in which net wheel displacement
    between the go cue and 20~ms before onset matched the final recorded choice
    (blue), and in which wheel displacement during the first 50~ms after movement
    onset matched the final recorded choice (green). Brackets report
    session interquartile ranges and the dashed line denotes 50\%. Pre-onset net
    displacement was close to chance with respect to final choice, whereas the
    movement beginning at the reported onset usually matched the final choice.}
    \label{fig:subthreshold-wheel}
\end{figure}
